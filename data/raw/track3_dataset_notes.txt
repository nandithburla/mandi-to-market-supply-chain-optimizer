Track 3: AgriTech - Mandi-to-Market Supply Chain Optimizer

This bundle contains synthetic agricultural supply chain data designed for the TransOrg AgentIQ Datathon.

Files included:
1. track3_mandi_arrivals.csv
   - Daily crop arrivals at local Mandis
   - Messy crop names (English/Hindi/Punjabi), mixed quantity units (Quintals, KG, Tonnes), messy dates

2. track3_price_and_msp.json
   - Wholesale prices and Minimum Support Price (MSP)
   - Messy currency formats, missing district/mandi IDs, string-based prices

3. track3_weather_sensors.xlsx
   - IoT weather sensor logs (Temperature, Rainfall, Humidity)
   - Mixed timezones (UTC vs IST), mixed temperature units (Celsius vs Fahrenheit), mixed rainfall units (mm vs inches)

4. track3_transport_logistics.csv
   - Truck transit logs from Mandis to Warehouses
   - Negative transit times, mixed distance units (KM vs Miles), messy vehicle registration numbers

5. track3_mandi_master.csv
   - Master data for Mandis and Districts
   - Messy mandi IDs, missing districts, inconsistent mandi types

6. track3_dataset_notes.txt
   - This file

Expected join keys:
- Mandi arrivals + Mandi master: mandi_id
- Prices + Mandi master: mandi_id or district
- Transport + Mandi master: mandi_id
- Weather sensors: Needs to be aggregated by date and mapped to districts/mandis via the master table (sensor location mapping is implied or can be assumed 1:1 with districts for simplicity).

Expected student tasks:
1. Standardize crop names (e.g., map "Wheat", "गेहूं", "Gehun" to a single canonical name).
2. Standardize mandi_id formats (MANDI001, MANDI-001, mandi_001, M001).
3. Parse and convert all quantity units to a standard metric (e.g., Quintals). 1 Tonne = 10 Qtl, 1 Qtl = 100 KG.
4. Parse and convert all distance units to Kilometers.
5. Clean price strings by removing currency symbols (₹, Rs., INR) and commas.
6. Handle timezone conversions in weather data (Convert UTC to IST).
7. Convert Fahrenheit temperatures to Celsius.
8. Convert inches of rainfall to millimeters.
9. Identify and handle impossible transit times (negative hours) and missing timestamps.
10. Standardize vehicle registration numbers.
11. Build an agricultural supply chain and price discovery dashboard.
12. Optionally build a graph-first AI agent.

Expected business metrics:
- Total crop arrivals (in Quintals)
- Average wholesale modal price vs MSP
- Price crash instances (Modal Price < MSP)
- Average transit time by warehouse
- Transit delay rate
- Weather impact on arrivals (Correlation between rainfall and arrival volume)
- Top Mandis by arrival volume
- Crop-wise arrival distribution

Example dashboard questions:
- Daily arrival trend of Wheat in Punjab Mandis.
- Which Mandis are experiencing prices below MSP?
- Average transit time from Mandi to WH-North.
- Correlation between heavy rainfall days and crop arrival drops.
- Top 5 Mandis by total arrival volume this month.
- Transport routes with the highest delay frequency.

Example agent queries:
- Plot the daily arrival trend of Wheat in Amritsar mandi vs MSP for the last 30 days.
- Show total arrivals by crop type.
- Which mandi has the highest average transit delay?
- Compare total rainfall by district over the last 3 months.
- Show the distribution of wholesale prices for Rice.
- Which warehouse receives the highest volume of crops?

All data is synthetic and generated only for educational/datathon purposes.
